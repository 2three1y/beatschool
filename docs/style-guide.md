# Style guide: the target sound

What "slaps" means in this course: **dark-but-cute underground** (pluggnb-adjacent, rage-lite, digicore, jerk). A dark lead loop with a half-step rub over a deep plain 808, OR a cute major-key glide lead, with bounce in the drums and an ending that stops dead.

These rules came from measuring reference tracks (tempo, levels, 808 share, drum grids; analysis only, no audio kept) and from producer tutorials, rewritten in our own words.

## Tempo and key
- Home tempo **140–145**. Underground/jerk 144–155. Digicore 150–166+. New jazz about 125–130.
- Dark lane: **C# or D minor**, with the **C#–D half-step rub**. 808 roots around C#1/D1 (35–37 Hz), with C1, F#1, G#1 nearby.
- Cute lane: major keys (G#, C#, D). Write progressions as **bass notes only**: 6-1-4-5, 6-4-1-5, 4-5-6-1.
- No pretty maj7/m9 lofi chords in the dark lane.

## Drums
- Sparse 8th hats **with holes** around the clap. One skippy 32nd double per bar. **At most one short roll per 8 bars.**
- **Syncopated clap**, thin, not a plain 2-and-4.
- In the dark lane the 808 IS the kick. In jerk/jersey switch-ups the kick IS the bounce.
- **The bounce:** low end on 16ths **0, 6, 12 | 4, 10** over 2 bars.

## 808
- Long, plain, mono, low. About 80–94 % of the energy in the references.
- Busy (slides, octave pops, half-step wiggles) in ONE section only.
- Saturate it enough that 100–400 Hz harmonics carry it on a phone speaker.
- Drop 2: louder, dirtier, more octave pops.

## Melody
- Stacked and dense, never one clean sine: detuned saw stabs (5–7 voices), square plucks, bitcrushed squares.
- Glide leads: mono, 20–30 ms portamento, a short note right before each slide.
- Two leads talking: lead 2 only plays in lead 1's gaps.
- Anchor notes (locked) + free notes (seeded random, then curated).
- Texture trick: write it simple, wreck it, granulate it, keep the bar that sounds like magic (stage 07).

## FX and space
- Few FX. Transitions are **hard mutes on the bar line**. **Silence is the riser**: 2 beats of nothing before the drop.
- Filter snap (low-passed about 1.5 kHz, then open) and width snap (narrow, then wide) are "free new".
- Ping-pong delay on plucks and answers. Reverb mostly on pads, stabs and the main pluck.
- Stutters and tape stops: 1 or 2 per track, max.

## Arrangement
- Start on the hook, or a short filtered intro (≤ 8 bars) with **the 808 landing on bar 9**.
- Two-stage drops. A perc pickup 2 bars before a drop.
- **Never 16 bars untouched.** Change one element at a time.
- **The switch-up must be the coolest part:** new drums, new 808 pattern, new melodic idea. Never the same loop bitcrushed.
- **Drop 2 > drop 1:** +1–2 LU, new layer or octave-up lead, wider, dirtier 808.
- Ending: drums leave, the hook is exposed, **dead stop on the bar line**. No fades.
- About 1:20–2:15. Six to eight 8-bar blocks.
- It should still slap slowed (0.8×) and sped up (1.25×).

## Loudness
Integrated about **−9 LUFS**, drop crest 6–9 dB, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP, intro 6–10 LU under the drop, mono sub.
